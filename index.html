How it Works

Three simple steps to go from feature to test cases.

1

Describe a Feature

Type or paste a feature description — for example, "User login with email and password" — or pick a random story to get started quickly.

2

Generate Test Cases

Click "Generate" and the AI creates 4–6 structured test cases with title, description, steps, expected results, priority, and tags.

3

Review & Export

Mark each test case as pass/fail/blocked, sort by priority, save to projects, and export in your preferred format.
